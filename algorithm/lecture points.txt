Algorithm

What is algorithm
	An algorithm is step by step procedure used  to solve problem or perform specific task

eg. Write an algorithm to calculate addition of two numbers
	
	1. start
	2. read two  numbers A & B
	3. calculate total=A+B
	4. display total
	5. stop

Write an algorithm to find the largest of two numbers
	

A good algorithm generally has these characteristics:
Characteristic		Meaning
Input			Accepts zero or more inputs.
Output			Produces at least one result.
Definiteness		Every step is clear and unambiguous.
Finiteness		Finishes after a finite number of steps.
Effectiveness		Each step is practical and executable.
